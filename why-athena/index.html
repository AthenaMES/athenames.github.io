What makes Athena different

One operational picture. Even when work takes different paths.

Shaped by the people who run the floor

Athena was developed inside an operating factory. Every screen started as a real need from someone who plans, supervises or runs a machine.

The real job is never a straight line

Split quantities, alternate machines, rework and vendor delays are part of normal work. Athena records them as steps, so no piece goes missing.

No sensors. No machine connections.

Athena does not need IoT devices or any new hardware on your machines. Your operators record their work on a computer you already have, kept in each production area.
