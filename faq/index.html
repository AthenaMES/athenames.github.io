FAQ

Clear answers, from the start.

Why not do this inside our ERP?

An ERP is built for orders, stock and accounts, and usually records production after it happens. Athena follows each job card through every stage as it happens, with split lots, rework, vendor delays and machine changes. You keep your ERP; Athena takes job cards from it.

Do we need barcode scanners or IoT devices?

No. Operators and supervisors record each action on a computer in their production area, and Athena's rules update the picture. It shows what your team records; it does not sense movements nobody entered.

Is OEE captured automatically?

No. OEE comes from production, run time and stop reasons entered by operators. It is not live machine telemetry, so no sensors are needed.

Can we build our own reports?

Yes. Your team picks the fields, filters and layout for the reports they need, and can export them to Excel.

Does Athena work with our existing ERP?

Today job cards come in through Excel or CSV files. Any specific mapping or a direct link is reviewed during discovery.

Does it work in our language?

Yes. Labels, stop reasons and messages can be set up in the language your team uses.

How does implementation work?

We map your process in detail, set Athena up for your plant, train every role on the shop floor, go live zone by zone, and support you after go-live.

How is it priced and deployed?

By proposal, based on your plant size and the modules you need. Local server or cloud is decided together during discovery.
