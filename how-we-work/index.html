How we work with you

We don't hand over software. We implement it with you.

Every plant runs differently, so every implementation starts on your shop floor and stays with you after go-live.

  1. Detailed process mapping

    We walk your floor and map every route, stage, vendor, report and variation, with the people who run them.

  2. Setup and configuration

    Items, routes, machines, reasons, roles and reports set up for your plant. Data brought in from your ERP and Excel.

  3. Hands-on training

    On the shop floor, role by role: operators, supervisors, PPC, quality, stores and management.

  4. Go-live, zone by zone

    Start with one zone, check the numbers together, then expand stage by stage at a pace your team can handle.

  5. End-to-end support

    We stay after go-live: reviews, fixes and changes as your process changes.

Built by manufacturers, for manufacturers. We speak job cards, routes and dispatch dates, so your team talks to people who have run a shop floor.
